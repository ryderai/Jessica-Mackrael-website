Buying a Home Near Eglin AFB

Eglin’s land range alone covers 724 square miles. The right town depends on which side of the base you work on.

Updated October 12, 2026 · By Jessica Mackrael, Realtor® and Military Relocation Professional (MRP)

The Short Answer

Which town is closest to Eglin?

It depends on the gate. From Valparaiso and Niceville, Eglin’s East Gate is about 4 and 9 minutes away. From Shalimar and Fort Walton Beach, the State Road 85 side of the base is about 4 and 11 minutes.

Crestview is about 26–29 minutes from the East Gate and the SR 85 side, and about 19 minutes from Duke Field. (Google Maps, checked October 12, 2026.)

The Installation

About Eglin, Duke Field and the 7th Group

The 96th Test Wing runs the base — its commander is the installation commander. Its land range covers 724 square miles, and the base supports about 20,000 military and civilian personnel and 43,000 dependents, according to the 96th Test Wing fact sheet.

Duke Field, home of the 919th Special Operations Wing, is about three miles south of downtown Crestview and about 12 miles north of Eglin’s East Gate (Wikipedia). The Army’s 7th Special Forces Group is based on the north end of the Eglin reservation, about six miles south of Crestview (Wikipedia).

Town by Town

The towns around Eglin

Niceville and Valparaiso — the East Gate side

Niceville sits on Boggy Bayou, which opens into Choctawhatchee Bay (Wikipedia); Valparaiso is next door. Both put the East Gate under 10 minutes away. Niceville had the highest recent median of the towns in the sales table below: $532,800 (3 months to Aug. 2026, Redfin).

Shalimar and Fort Walton Beach — the SR 85 side

Shalimar is about 4 minutes from the Armament Museum visitor center on State Road 85, and Fort Walton Beach about 11. Fort Walton Beach is also about 13 minutes from Hurlburt Field. Its median sale was $329,843 (3 months to Sept. 2026, Redfin).

Crestview — north, near Duke Field

Crestview is about 19 minutes from Duke Field and 26–29 minutes from the East Gate and the SR 85 side. Its median sale was $277,318 (3 months to Sept. 2026) — the lowest of the towns in the sales table below. See the Crestview guide and Niceville vs. Crestview.

Mary Esther and Navarre — if one of you works at Hurlburt

These towns trade a longer Eglin drive for a short one to Hurlburt Field. See the Hurlburt Field guide.

Schools

Schools near Eglin

Niceville, Valparaiso, Shalimar, Fort Walton Beach and Crestview are all in the Okaloosa County School District, which earned an A for the 2025–26 school year (WEAR-TV, July 1, 2026). Navarre is in Santa Rosa County’s district, which also earned an A.

Confirm a home’s school zoning with the district before you make an offer. Eglin’s School Liaison can help with transfers and enrollment.

Before You Buy

Five things to check before you buy near Eglin

  • Check the flood zone for the exact address on FEMA’s Flood Map Service Center. Flood insurance can change a monthly budget more than a small price difference.
  • Look up the tax history on the Okaloosa County Property Appraiser site. In Florida, the seller’s tax bill is often not what you’ll pay: assessment limits on a homestead reset when a home sells.
  • Know your housing allowance. Look up your rate on the Defense Department’s BAH lookup, or try Jessica’s BAH calculator.
  • Using a VA loan? Start with the VA’s home loan page and get your Certificate of Eligibility (the VA paper that shows you qualify) early.
  • Drive the commute at the time you’ll really leave for work before you commit — traffic at 7 a.m. is not the same as at noon.

Buying in Navarre? Use the Santa Rosa County Property Appraiser instead of Okaloosa’s.

Questions & Answers

Eglin AFB housing questions

What is the closest town to Eglin AFB?
It depends on the gate. Valparaiso was about 4 minutes from the East Gate, and Shalimar about 4 minutes from the SR 85 side of the base (Google Maps, checked October 12, 2026). Niceville was about 9 minutes from the East Gate.
How long is the drive from Crestview to Eglin?
About 26 minutes to the SR 85 side and 29 minutes to the East Gate from central Crestview, both on State Road 85. Duke Field was about 19 minutes.
Where is Duke Field?
About three miles south of downtown Crestview and about 12 miles north of Eglin’s East Gate. It is home to the 919th Special Operations Wing.
Which school district serves Eglin families?
Most towns around Eglin are in the Okaloosa County School District, which earned an A for 2025–26. Navarre is in Santa Rosa County’s district, which also earned an A.
How do I find my BAH for Eglin?
Use the Defense Department’s BAH rate lookup with your duty station ZIP code and rank, or try the BAH calculator on Jessica’s Military Relocation page.
Can I buy near Eglin before I arrive on orders?
Yes. Jessica does live video tours from the house, and helps coordinate the inspection, appraisal and closing while you're at your current duty station, and works to time the closing around your report date (the day you must report to your new base).
