Area Guide · Crestview

Living in Crestview, Florida

Okaloosa County’s largest city, where I-10 meets State Road 85 — and where a budget usually buys more house than it does near the water.

Updated October 12, 2026 · By Jessica Mackrael, Realtor® and Military Relocation Professional (MRP)

The Short Answer

Is Crestview a good place to buy?

For a lot of buyers, yes — if the drive works for you. Crestview homes sold for a median of about $277K over the three months to September 2026, at a median of $166 per square foot. That is well below Fort Walton Beach ($211), Navarre ($215) and Niceville ($287), according to Redfin.

The trade-off is distance. From central Crestview, Duke Field is about 19 minutes away and Eglin’s East Gate about 29. Hurlburt Field is about 43. All of those drives run south on State Road 85.

Crestview homes have also been taking longer to sell — a median of 84 days, up from 71 a year earlier, per Redfin. For a buyer, more time on the market often means more room to negotiate on price, repairs or closing costs.

Is It a Fit?

Who Crestview suits — and who should look elsewhere

A good fit if you…

  • are assigned to Duke Field or the 7th Special Forces Group
  • work on the north end of the Eglin range
  • want more square footage or a bigger lot for your budget
  • want quick access to I-10 for trips east or west

Think twice if you…

  • are assigned to Hurlburt Field — about 43 minutes each way
  • want to live near the bay or the beach
  • work on the south side of Eglin and want the shortest drive — Niceville and Valparaiso are under 10 minutes from the East Gate
Schools

Schools in Crestview

Crestview’s public schools are part of the Okaloosa County School District, which earned an A from the Florida Department of Education for the 2025–26 school year, as reported by WEAR-TV on July 1, 2026. Crestview High School is in the city.

Which school a home is zoned for depends on its exact address, so confirm the zoning with the district before you make an offer. Military families can also ask their base’s School Liaison for help with transfers and enrollment.

Before You Buy

Five things to check before you buy in Crestview

  • Check the flood zone for the exact address on FEMA’s Flood Map Service Center. Flood insurance can change a monthly budget more than a small price difference.
  • Look up the tax history on the Okaloosa County Property Appraiser site. In Florida, the seller’s tax bill is often not what you’ll pay: assessment limits on a homestead reset when a home sells.
  • Know your housing allowance. Look up your rate on the Defense Department’s BAH lookup, or try Jessica’s BAH calculator.
  • Using a VA loan? Start with the VA’s home loan page and get your Certificate of Eligibility (the VA paper that shows you qualify) early.
  • Drive the commute at the time you’ll really leave for work before you commit — traffic at 7 a.m. is not the same as at noon.

Questions & Answers

Crestview questions buyers ask

Is Crestview a good place to live if I’m stationed at Eglin?
It depends on where on Eglin you work. From central Crestview, Duke Field is about 19 minutes and Eglin’s East Gate about 29 minutes (Google Maps, checked October 12, 2026). What the drive buys you is price: Crestview’s median sale was about $277K (3 months to Sept. 2026), against about $533K in Niceville (3 months to Aug. 2026), per Redfin.
How far is Crestview from Hurlburt Field?
About 32 miles — roughly 43 minutes from central Crestview to Hurlburt’s main gate on Google Maps, checked October 12, 2026. Fort Walton Beach and Mary Esther are much closer to Hurlburt.
Are homes cheaper in Crestview than in Niceville?
Yes, by recent sales. Redfin shows a Crestview median of $277,318 (3 months to Sept. 2026) and a Niceville median of $532,800 (3 months to Aug. 2026). Per square foot it was $166 in Crestview and $287 in Niceville.
What county is Crestview in?
Okaloosa County. Crestview is the county seat and the county’s largest city, with 27,134 people in the 2020 Census.
How long do homes take to sell in Crestview?
A median of 84 days for the three months to September 2026, up from 71 days a year earlier, according to Redfin.
