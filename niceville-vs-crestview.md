# Niceville vs. Crestview

Two Okaloosa County towns, about 19 miles apart by road, that answer the same question — where to live near Eglin — in very different ways.

_Updated October 12, 2026 · By Jessica Mackrael, Realtor® and Military Relocation Professional (MRP)_

## Which should I choose?

**Choose Niceville** if a short drive to Eglin’s East Gate and life on the bay matter most. It’s about 9 minutes to the gate, and homes sold for a median of about $533K (3 months to Aug. 2026, [Redfin](https://www.redfin.com/city/12482/FL/Niceville/housing-market)).

**Choose Crestview** if you want more home for the money. Homes sold for a median of about $277K (3 months to Sept. 2026) — but the East Gate is about 29 minutes away (Google Maps, checked October 12, 2026). Duke Field sits about three miles south of downtown Crestview ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_Field)); Google Maps timed both towns at under 20 minutes to its point inside the base.

## The same budget, two very different houses

At each town’s median price per square foot, a **$400,000** budget works out to roughly **2,410 square feet in Crestview** and roughly **1,390 in Niceville**.

That is rough math on Redfin’s medians, not a quote for any one home — age, condition, lot and water access all move the price. But it shows the size of the gap you’re choosing between.

## Who each town suits

### Niceville suits you if you…

- work on the East Gate side of Eglin (about 9 minutes)
- want to live near the water
- would rather pay more for a shorter commute
- want a quicker drive to Hurlburt than Crestview gives (about 29 vs. 43 minutes)

### Crestview suits you if you…

- want more square footage or a bigger lot for the money
- are assigned to Duke Field or the 7th Special Forces Group
- want I-10 close by
- don’t mind about 29 minutes to Eglin’s East Gate

## Niceville vs. Crestview questions

### Is Niceville or Crestview better for Eglin AFB?

For the East Gate side of Eglin, Niceville — about 9 minutes against 29 from Crestview (Google Maps, checked October 12, 2026). Duke Field sits about three miles south of downtown Crestview, and Google Maps timed both towns at under 20 minutes to its point inside the base. Crestview’s recent median sale price was much lower.

### Is Crestview cheaper than Niceville?

Yes, on recent sales. Redfin shows a Crestview median of $277,318 (3 months to Sept. 2026) and a Niceville median of $532,800 (3 months to Aug. 2026) — $166 vs. $287 per square foot.

### How far apart are Niceville and Crestview?

About 19 miles, or roughly 26 minutes by car on State Road 85 (Google Maps, checked October 12, 2026).

### Do Niceville and Crestview have the same school district?

Yes. Both are in the Okaloosa County School District, which earned an A for 2025–26. Zoning goes by exact address, so confirm it before you make an offer.

### Where do homes sell faster, Niceville or Crestview?

Niceville, recently. The median was 68 days on market in Niceville (3 months to Aug. 2026) and 84 in Crestview (3 months to Sept. 2026), per Redfin.
